## The ideas, ranked again

1. **Pressure that grows with success** (new, M; new step 26, built next).
   - **What.** Demand keeps rising as the network does well, so busier trains, fuller platforms and the roads' queues come back late in the game, at a level that's designed rather than accidental. Three sources, all real:
     - **The city's jobs come back.** The census was taken in lockdown and understates the city centre's jobs (`docs/DESIGN.md`, Homes and draw). Piccadilly's draw rises day by day towards a normal year's figure, from the ONS Business Register and Employment Survey (BRES, by area on Nomis). So the morning peak into Manchester keeps growing.
     - **Homes grow faster and further where the line is good.** The ceiling rises with what's bought nearby: Homes that grow, with regeneration from the signature stages of step 18.
     - **Surges you can see**, moved up from step 23: match days at Edgeley Park, Stepping Hill Hospital's shift changes at Woodsmoor, Christmas shopping at Merseyway. Each is a seeded peak the player can prepare for.
   - **Designed pressure.** A new band in the baselines: the share lost at each peak from day 4 on stays within a range, about 2 to 6%. The bot then measures that pressure is there, not only that pacing holds.
   - **Why.** It puts the problem back into the late game, so the fixes (frequencies, longer trains, stage tiers, flyovers, the feeders) are bought because they're needed. Serves: an incremental game, pressure on the map, depth before breadth.
   - **What it changes.** `draw` growth and `grow` in `src/sim/sim.ts`, the census tool for BRES, Life's match days (from crowd to demand), the baselines.
   - **Risk.** Pacing, by design. The bot runs at 16 and 32 h.
   - **Call taken.** Demand grows. The band is proposed at 2 to 6% and set by the build from the bot.
2. **Stages that keep going** (step 18, unchanged, second to build). Late cash still reaches £300,000 with two cards left at Stockport. Its tiers are now also the fixes for step 26's pressure, so it follows 26.
3. **Gridlock that keeps moving** (step 20). The frequencies took drives off from 15–35% to 29–49%, so the roads now clear more as the player builds. G5 still matters: step 26's growing demand needs the roads to push it onto the trains. Unchanged in order: after G4.
4. **Feeder buses first** (step 21), then **the Bee Network** (step 22). Unchanged.
5. **Late trains cost money** (step 19, made smaller).
   - With every train on time late in the game, Delay Repay would cost nothing. Its red coins would never show.
   - **Call taken:** build only its punctuality part now, putting the share of trains within 5 minutes in the contract's reputation and on the Network card.
   - Refunds wait until step 26 brings lateness back, and only if the bot shows it does.
