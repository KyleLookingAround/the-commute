# Station reference photos

Photographs of the real stations, from several angles, for anyone building or checking a kit, an audit or a line out. Look here before searching again.

## How to use it

- One folder per station, named by its id in `src/data/network.json` (or the obvious id for a station not in the game yet).
- Each folder's `index.md` lists every photo: what it shows and from where, when it was taken, who took it, the licence and links to the source page and the full image. Below the table, **What the photos settle** answers questions the kits have left open, and **Still unknown** says what no free photograph shows.
- A station's photos of its surroundings are named `area-<what>-<n>.jpg` and have their own section, **Round the station**, in its `index.md`.
- Photos are JPEGs at most 800 px on the long edge, named by angle (`platform-1-1.jpg`, `footbridge-1.jpg`, `entrance-1.jpg`, `overview-1.jpg`). `platform-view-n` means a view along a platform whose number the source doesn't give.

## Licences and credits

- Every photo is freely licensed and credited in its station's `index.md`. Those from geograph.org.uk are © their photographers under [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/); reuse them with the photographer's name, the licence and a link to the source page.
- Only freely licensed photos are kept (CC BY, CC BY-SA, public domain). Where a licence was unclear, the photo was left out.
