# Ardwick

The real Ardwick is one island platform on the Hope Valley and Glossop lines beside the main line out of Piccadilly, reached by an old riveted footbridge from a gate in the viaduct wall on Blind Lane. In the game it serves the stopper with a platform each side (a game choice).

## What the photos settle

- The footbridge: riveted girders under a diamond-lattice parapet, rust brown, open, its stairs in straight flights with a landing on a lattice trestle on a brick base (footbridge-1 to -3), as the kit's `lattice` and `flights` parts draw it.
- One small blue shelter on the island and blue benches; white lamp columns (platform-2).
- The entrance is a gate in the viaduct wall on cobbled Blind Lane, with no building; the only building is the disused staff building outside (entrance-1, area-staff-building-1): the kit's brick booking hut is the game's.
- No car park, cycle stands or ticket office in any photo.
