# The A6 beside the line

The A6 runs beside the line all the way: London Road and Buxton Road through Hazel Grove and Great Moor, Wellington Road South and North through Stockport (high over Mersey Square), on through Heaton Chapel, then Stockport Road through Levenshulme, Longsight and Ardwick to London Road at Piccadilly. The 192 bus runs along it the whole way, every few minutes, between Hazel Grove (and Stepping Hill Hospital) and Piccadilly. Other photos of it: Woodsmoor's `area-a6-1`, Heaton Chapel's `area-a6-1`, Levenshulme's `area-stockport-road-1`, the town centre's `wellington-road-south-1` and `town-hall-1`, and the Piccadilly approach's `london-road-1`.

## For the map

- Hazel Grove (London Road, Buxton Road): a single carriageway, about 12 to 15 m kerb to kerb, guard railings at the junctions; two-storey red-brick shops, some with mock-Tudor gables, banks on the corners, houses set back behind hedges. The 192 turns at Stepping Hill Hospital and at the Rising Sun.
- Stockport (Wellington Road South and North): wide, two lanes each way with a central reservation and bus lanes, about 20 to 25 m; it crosses Mersey Square high on its own arched bridge; the Town Hall (white stone), the Plaza, the LNWR warehouse (long red brick) and offices and Grand Central line it.
- Heaton Chapel (Wellington Road North): two lanes each way in places, a red-surfaced lane, Victorian pubs (the Chapel House) and churches (St Thomas's), semis and villas set back behind hedges and trees.
- Levenshulme (Stockport Road): a single carriageway with parking bays and bus lanes, about 15 m; continuous two-storey brick shop terraces with flats over, the 1898 council offices (the Antiques Village) with its clock turret.
- Longsight and Ardwick (Stockport Road): wider, bus lanes and a cycle lane behind the bus shelters; newer brick houses and flats, parks (Crowcroft Park, Ardwick Green) behind railings.
- London Road at Piccadilly: a city street of three or four lanes and tram tracks, tall glass and stone offices and the station's car park, the old fire station and Victorian blocks opposite.
- The 192: Stagecoach double-deckers in blue, red and orange, nearly every stop along the way; bus lanes are red-surfaced in places.

## What the photos settle

- The A6 is lined the whole way with two-storey shops and houses, wider and more open from Longsight in (levenshulme-1, longsight-192-1, ardwick-longsight-1).
- It runs high over Mersey Square in Stockport (stockport-wellington-road-north-1).
- The 192 runs it from Stepping Hill to Piccadilly (stepping-hill-192-1, longsight-192-1, london-road-1).
