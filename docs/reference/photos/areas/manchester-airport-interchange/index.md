# Manchester Airport station and its transport interchange

The Station, between Terminals 1, 2 and 3: the railway and Metrolink platforms lie side by side in a cutting under curved steel roofs; the bus and coach station is on the level above at ground level; above that, the covered Skylink walkways run out to the terminals and the Radisson hotel. See the station's own folder for the platforms and the junction.

## For the map

- Three levels: rail and tram platforms in the cutting; the bus and coach station at ground level over it; Skylink walkways above, fanning out to T1, T2 and T3.
- The station roofs are long, low curves of grey steel on round white columns; the interchange has a curved canopy and glass walls.
- The bus station is a wide, open tarmac apron with lettered bays (A to L or so), yellow hatching and a glass canopy along one side, edged with brick walls and trees.
- Round it: glazed office blocks (about 5–8 storeys, blue glass), the Radisson Blu (about 10 storeys, grey cladding), multi-storey car parks, and the terminals' glazed fronts with deep canopies.
- Roads: Terminal Road North and South are two- to three-lane one-way roads with drop-off lanes, roundabouts and footways in block paving.

## What the photos settle

- The airport's bus and coach station is part of the same interchange as the railway and tram station, directly above them.
- Trams and trains have separate platforms side by side; the trams terminate at an island with two faces.
