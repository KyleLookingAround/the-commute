# Marple (town centre)

The town round Marple station: the station lies low in the Goyt valley at the bottom of Brabyns Brow (A626), and the centre is up the hill to the south-west, along Stockport Road (B6101) and the pedestrianised Market Street, with the Peak Forest Canal and its lock flight running between them. Marple has no bus station; buses stop along Stockport Road.

## For the map

- The station is at the foot of Brabyns Brow, a wooded two-lane A road that climbs south-west from the Goyt valley over the canal into town; nothing but trees and a few houses line it near the station.
- The centre is a small market town: two- and three-storey buildings, mostly red brick or stone with slate roofs, some white-painted; shops at ground floor, flats above.
- Market Street is pedestrianised, block-paved, about 10 m between frontages, with benches, planters and bunting.
- Stockport Road (B6101) is a two-lane high street with parking bays and bus stops; the Regent cinema (white, arched windows) is a landmark on it.
- The Peak Forest Canal runs through the town with a steep lock flight and stone basins; the canal crosses the railway north of the station on a tunnel and an aqueduct over the Goyt.

## What the photos settle

- Marple has no bus station: buses stop on Stockport Road.
- The town centre is not beside the station; the walk is up Brabyns Brow and over the canal.
