# Piccadilly approach

The streets round Manchester Piccadilly: Station Approach and its forecourt off London Road (the A6), Gateway House curving along it, Fairfield Street under the 13/14 viaduct with the taxi rank, and the multi-storey car park. The station itself is in [../../piccadilly/](../../piccadilly/index.md).

## For the map

- Station Approach: a short rise off London Road to the concourse front, buses and taxis only, a wide paved forecourt with planters and cycle stands in front of the glass concourse wall.
- Gateway House: a long S-curved slab along the approach's north-east side, nine storeys of silver horizontal bands and ribbon glazing, shops at the foot.
- Piccadilly Place: beige stone and glass office blocks across the approach on the south-west side, about eight to ten storeys.
- London Road (the A6): a wide dual road with the tram tracks crossing it at the approach; red-brick and terracotta Victorian blocks and the old London Road Fire Station (red brick with a corner tower) on its far side, newer towers further south.
- Fairfield Street runs under the 13/14 viaduct on a steel girder bridge; the taxi rank is beside the station on it.
- The multi-storey car park is clad in rust-red weathering steel panels.

## What the photos settle

- The car park's cladding is weathering steel in a random pattern (carpark-1), as the kit draws it.
- Gateway House's curve and banding (station-approach-1 and -2).
- The taxi rank is on Fairfield Street, not on Station Approach (fairfield-street-3), which carries only buses now.
