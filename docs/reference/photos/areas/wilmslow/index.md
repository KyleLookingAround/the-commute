# Wilmslow (town centre)

The town round Wilmslow station: the station sits on an embankment at the edge of the centre, with Station Road passing under the line on a girder bridge and leading into the centre. The centre is compact, pedestrianised Grove Street with brick shops and offices round it; St Bartholomew's church and the River Bollin lie to the north, and the A34 bypass runs in a deep cutting close by. There is no bus station; buses stop on the station approach.

## For the map

- The station's town side faces onto a forecourt and car park (see the station's folder); Station Road runs under the line beside it and into town.
- Buildings: mostly two- to three-storey red brick with slate or tile roofs, a few black-and-white timbered fronts, and modern brick office blocks with glazed gables; few above four storeys.
- Grove Street is pedestrianised, red block paving, about 10–12 m between shop fronts, with lamp posts, hanging baskets and planters.
- St Bartholomew's is red sandstone with a battlemented tower, beside the Bollin valley's trees north of the centre.
- The A34 bypass is a four-lane dual carriageway in a deep walled cutting close to the centre (which side of the station, the photos do not show); the A538 Manchester Road heads north towards the airport.

## What the photos settle

- Wilmslow has no bus station: the buses stop on the station approach (see the station's busstop photo).
- The station is on an embankment; roads pass under it.
