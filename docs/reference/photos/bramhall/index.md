# Bramhall

Two side platforms on the electrified Stockport–Macclesfield line (the Stoke line) between Cheadle Hulme and Poynton, on an embankment. A green girder bridge carries the line over Bramhall Road South at the station; the platforms have only bus-stop shelters, with a white panelled fence along the back.

## What the photos settle

- Bramhall has no station building on the platforms: just glass shelters.
- There is a car park, beside the line, visible from the platform.
- The line crosses Bramhall Road South on a green girder bridge at the station, and access is up from the road.
