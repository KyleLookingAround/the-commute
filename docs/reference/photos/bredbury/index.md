# Bredbury

Two side platforms on an embankment on the Manchester–New Mills line, just east of the bridge over the A560 Stockport Road East. Each platform has a modern brick building with a pitched grey roof; a green footbridge crosses at the Manchester end; the old station house beside it is now a private house.

## Round the station

The station stands on an embankment beside the A560 Stockport Road East, which passes under the line on a low bridge; the Rising Sun pub is at the foot of the station steps, and new housing on the Bredbury Curve has a second way in from Kennett Drive.

## What the photos settle

- Pedestrian-only entrance by the Rising Sun on Stockport Road East, up steps; no car park in view on that side.
- Both platforms have a brick building; neither has a long canopy beyond the building's own.
- The footbridge is a green-painted steel span at the Manchester end, not a subway.
- There is a station car park (area-carpark-1).
