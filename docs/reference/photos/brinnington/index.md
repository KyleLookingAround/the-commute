# Brinnington

A 1977 station in a tree-lined cutting on the Manchester–New Mills line: two side platforms, a brick booking office at street level on The Link, a blue footbridge, and The Link's road bridge over the line at the station.

## What the photos settle

- Street level is above the platforms: the booking office stands at road level beside the bridge, and the platforms lie in a cutting below.
- Two side platforms, no island; the way across is the blue footbridge, plus the road bridge.
- Shelters are small brick boxes, not canopies.
- There is no car park in view: kerbside parking only by the entrance.
