# Burnage

Two side platforms on an embankment on the Styal line, where the line crosses Fog Lane on a brick-abutted girder bridge just south of the station. A small flat-roofed brick building with a deep canopy stands on one platform; each platform has a ramped entrance from Fog Lane.

## What the photos settle

- Each platform has its own ramp from Fog Lane: step-free, no footbridge.
- There is a small station building with a deep flat canopy on one platform; the other has a shelter.
- Fog Lane crosses under the line just south of the station.

## Still unknown
