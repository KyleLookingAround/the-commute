# Chapel-en-le-Frith

Two side platforms on the Buxton line well outside the town, up a tree-lined lane: a stone station building of 1867 with tall chimneys on one platform, a signal box with semaphore signals, and a foot crossing over the line to the Manchester-bound platform. Not in the game yet.

## What the photos settle

- A stone building with tall chimneys on one platform (building-1 and -2).
- A signal box with semaphores, and a foot crossing rather than a footbridge (signal-box-1, foot-crossing-1).
