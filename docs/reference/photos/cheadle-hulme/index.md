# Cheadle Hulme

The junction station where the Crewe line (via Handforth and Wilmslow) and the Macclesfield and Stoke line (via Bramhall) part. Four platforms: 1 and 2 on the Crewe lines, 3 and 4 on the Stoke lines, the two pairs side by side in a V and each carried over Station Road (A5149) on its own bridge. Electrified, with a white lattice footbridge linking all four and a red-brick lift tower.

## Round the station

The station spans Station Road (A5149) on two bridges, one for each pair of lines; the old bank building stands between them, with shops, offices and the Waitrose car park round about.

## What the photos settle

- Cheadle Hulme has four platforms in two pairs on diverging lines: 1–2 on the Crewe lines, 3–4 on the Stoke lines; both pairs cross Station Road on separate bridges.
- The way across is one long lattice footbridge over all four tracks, with a brick lift tower: step-free.
- The street entrance is on Station Road beside the old brick station building; the Waitrose car park is opposite.
