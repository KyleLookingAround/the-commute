# Davenport

Two side platforms on the Hazel Grove line in a shallow cutting, with Bramhall Lane (the A5102) over the north end. The booking office stands on the bridge's south-east corner. Positive v is east, as in the kit.

## What the photos settle

- The booking office's roof is hipped, with red-and-white bargeboards under the eaves and a chimney (entrance-1, building-east-1): the `hipped` part is right; the trim colour is not drawn yet.
- The stair to the east platform is red and white, straight along the line beside the booking office (building-east-1, bridge-1); the steps to the west platform are on the far side of the bridge (bridge-1).
- The bridge is a brick arch under a red-and-white girder parapet (bridge-1, bridge-2).
- The west platform's canopy is flat, on a white-painted brick building; the east platform has only the small white shelter (platform-east-north-1): as the kit has it.
- There is a car park (area-carpark-1) and a 'Car Park' sign on Bramhall Lane by the booking office (area-bramhall-lane-1). The photo's grid references put it just west of the camera on the train, so probably the west side, but they are too coarse to settle it.
