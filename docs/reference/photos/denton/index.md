# Denton

On the Stockport–Denton–Guide Bridge line, served by a token weekly train. Once two island platforms and four tracks; now a single island platform between two lines, in a wide cutting, reached by steel stairs from the Manchester Road North bridge.

## Round the station

The only way in is from Manchester Road North (A57), which runs eastbound beside the M67; the westbound Manchester Road South is on the other side of the motorway.

## What the photos settle

- Denton is one island platform between two tracks, reached only by stairs from the road bridge.
- No building, no shelter of note, no car park.
