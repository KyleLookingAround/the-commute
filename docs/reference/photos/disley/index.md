# Disley

Two side platforms on the Buxton line in a wooded cutting just below the A6 (Buxton Road), with a tunnel mouth under Buxton Road at one end. The Buxton-bound platform is reached from the A6; a small car park and Station Approach are on the other side. Not in the game yet.

## What the photos settle

- Two side platforms with small shelters (a white flat-roofed one, a small brick one), blue lamp columns, and a stone tunnel mouth under Buxton Road at the end (platforms-1, tunnel-1, platform-tunnel-2).
- The way in from the A6 is a path down through the trees (approach-path-1, area-buxton-road-1); Station Approach has a small car park (area-station-approach-1).
