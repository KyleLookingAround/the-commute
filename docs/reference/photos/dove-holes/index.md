# Dove Holes

Two side platforms on the Buxton line high on the moors, with an unusual layout: no footbridge, the platforms reached from either side, and a stone road bridge (Station Road) over the line at one end. Not in the game yet.

## What the photos settle

- No footbridge: each platform has its own way in, and the road bridge is stone (overview-1, bridge-1).
- A small car park beside one platform (overview-1, entrance-1).
