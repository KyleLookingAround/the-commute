# East Didsbury

Two side platforms on an embankment on the Styal line where it crosses Wilmslow Road (A5145) on bridge STY/32. Lightweight platforms with mesh fences, a small raised flat-roofed booking office on stilts by the bridge, and new flats along the town side. The Metrolink terminus is nearby.

## What the photos settle

- The station sits on an embankment by the Wilmslow Road bridge, with the booking office on stilts at platform level.
- No footbridge appears: the platforms are reached from the street, and Wilmslow Road is the way across.
