# Gatley

Two side platforms on an embankment on the Styal line between East Didsbury and Heald Green, where the line crosses Gatley Road (A560) on a girder bridge at the south end. A white-painted timber building with a hipped roof and canopy stands on one platform; a long ramped footbridge and an underpass from Cambridge Road link the sides.

## Round the station

The station is on Gatley Road (A560), which passes under the line at its south end, a short walk from Gatley village and the Tatton cinema building.

## What the photos settle

- Gatley keeps a timber station building with a hipped roof on one platform.
- The way across is a long ramped footbridge (step-free), plus an underpass at Cambridge Road.
- Gatley Road crosses under the line at the south end of the platforms.
