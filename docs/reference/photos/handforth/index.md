# Handforth

Two side platforms in a cutting on the Crewe line between Cheadle Hulme and Wilmslow. The ticket office stands at road level on the B5358 Station Road bridge at the north end of the platforms, with green-railed stairs down to each platform; the line is electrified. The station is known for its collection of heritage and foreign-language station signs.

## Round the station

The ticket office is on the Station Road (B5358) bridge; the Railway pub faces it, and Station Road meets Wilmslow Road at the Freemasons Arms a short walk west.

## What the photos settle

- The booking office is on the road bridge, at street level, not on a platform; the platforms are below in a cutting.
- The way across is the road bridge itself, with stairs down to each platform; no footbridge.
