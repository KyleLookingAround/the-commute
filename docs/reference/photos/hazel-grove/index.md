# Hazel Grove

Two side platforms where the wires end, the Buxton line running on south-east and the chord to the Hope Valley line leaving beyond Chester Road. Platform 1 (east, trains towards Buxton) has the signal box; platform 2 (west, towards Stockport) has the canopy. The building and the big car park are on the east side off Station Street. Positive v is east, as in the kit.

## What the photos settle

- The building is single-storey red brick under a shallow hipped roof (entrance-1): the  part is right.
- The footbridge is enclosed and glazed with a lift tower at each end (footbridge-1), and the north footbridge doesn't reach the platforms (footbridge-north-1): as the kit has them.
- The signal box is on platform 1, the east one (signal-box-1, platform-1-north-1), and the canopy on platform 2, the west (platform-2-1).
- The big car park is on the east side, between Station Street and the building (area-carpark-1 and -2).
- Buses stop on the approach by the building (area-approach-1).
