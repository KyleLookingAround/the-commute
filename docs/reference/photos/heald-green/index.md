# Heald Green

Two side platforms in a shallow cutting on the Styal line just north of the junction for Manchester Airport, beside Finney Lane's road bridge. A modern flat-roofed building stands on one platform, a green-railed ramped footbridge crosses the line, and a car park lies alongside.

## What the photos settle

- There is a station car park beside the line.
- The way across is a ramped green footbridge (step-free), and Finney Lane bridge runs over the north end.
- The building is a single-storey modern block with a flat canopy, on one platform.
