# Heaton Chapel

Two side platforms on the slow lines of the four-track main line in a deep cutting, Heaton Moor Road over the south end on brick arches. The west platform is the one towards Manchester. Positive v is east, as in the kit.

## What the photos settle

- Heaton Moor Road's bridge is dark brick on segmental arches with thick piers (bridge-1, bridge-2), and the platform's ramp runs under a smaller arch at its end (ramp-1).
- The brick buildings step up against the cutting by the bridge (building-1, building-2).
- The shelters are curved-roofed on blue posts (shelter-1), which the kit draws as barrel-roofed shelters.
- No station car park shows, only cars parked on the side streets (area-tatton-road-north-1).
- There are small retail huts by the station on Heaton Moor Road (area-rail-and-ale-1).
