# Levenshulme

Two side platforms on the slow lines of the four-track main line, up on an embankment that crosses Albert Road on a bridge at the south end. Platform 1 (east) is for Stockport, platform 2 (west) for Manchester. The way in is from street level on the east side, off the Street With No Name, where Levenshulme Market is held in the car park. Positive v is east, as in the kit.

## What the photos settle

- The way in is a two-storey brick block at street level on the east side (entrance-1), as the kit has it.
- Platform 1 (east) has the long flat-roofed brick building and a barrel-roofed shelter with Northern-blue panels just north of it (platform-1-across-1, platform-1-building-1).
- There are arched recesses in the station's brick wall on Albert Road (area-albert-road-1); how many, and whether they run under the building, no photo shows.
- The car park off the Street With No Name is where the market is held (area-market-1 and -2).
- No canopy on either platform.

## Still unknown

- Platform 2's shelter, which only shows at the edges of photos.
- How many recesses the embankment wall has, and their height.
- The subway at the south end.
