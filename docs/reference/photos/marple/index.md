# Marple

Two platforms in a deep wooded cutting below Station Road (A626), on the Hope Valley line from Manchester to New Mills and Sheffield. A blue footbridge with two lift towers crosses mid-station; a brick building with offices stands on one platform; the large car park is on the east side, at platform level; at the north end the line runs under a stone arch bridge and into the short tunnel beneath the Peak Forest Canal.

## What the photos settle

- Marple has a big car park, on the side away from the main building, alongside the platform.
- The footbridge has lifts: a tower at each end, so step-free access to both platforms.
- The station sits in a cutting with the road above at the north end; the north end runs straight into an arch bridge and the canal tunnel.
