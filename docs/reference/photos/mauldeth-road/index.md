# Mauldeth Road

Two side platforms on an embankment on the electrified Styal line between Levenshulme (via Slade Lane junction) and Burnage, where the line crosses Mauldeth Road on a girder bridge. The platforms were rebuilt with mesh fences and modern lamps; each has its own entrance from the street, with no footbridge.

## What the photos settle

- Each platform has its own path from the street, so there is no footbridge; the road under the bridge is the way across.
- No station building: shelters and help points only.
- The line crosses Mauldeth Road on a girder bridge right at the station.
