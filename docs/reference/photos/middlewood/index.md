# Middlewood

A halt in the woods on the Buxton line beyond Hazel Grove, about 800 yards from a public road: two side platforms in a wooded cutting under the old Macclesfield line's bridge, which now carries the Middlewood Way. Passengers come down ramps from the Way or along a path beside the line. Not in the game yet.

## What the photos settle

- Two side platforms, no buildings but a small white shelter, lamp columns and low fences (platforms-1, platform-train-1).
- The way in is by ramps from the Middlewood Way above, which crosses the station on the old line's bridge (ramp-1, area-middlewood-way-1).

## Still unknown

- No road, car park or bus stop: none shows, as the station is reached only by paths.
- The bridge's structure close up from the platforms.
