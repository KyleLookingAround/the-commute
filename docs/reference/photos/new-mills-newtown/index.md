# New Mills Newtown

Two side platforms on the Buxton line at Newtown, below the A6, with a lattice footbridge between them and Albion Road (the A6015) crossing on a stone bridge at the west end. A car park lies beside the station. One of New Mills' two stations; Central is on the Hope Valley line. Not in the game yet.

- A white flat-roofed building and a glazed shelter on one platform (platforms-west-1, shelters-1).
- Albion Road crosses on a stone-and-steel bridge at the west end (bridge-albion-road-1), and the car park is beside the station (area-carpark-1).
