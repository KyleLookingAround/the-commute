# Manchester Piccadilly

The terminus: bays 1 to 12 under the listed glazed train shed, the concourse at their buffer ends facing Station Approach, and the through island 13/14 on the viaduct over Fairfield Street on the south-west side. Platform numbers here are the real ones (1 is on the north side, 13/14 on the south-west). The streets round it are in [../areas/piccadilly-approach/](../areas/piccadilly-approach/index.md).

## What the photos settle

- Real platform numbering: platform 1 is the north side of the shed (shed-2) and 13/14 are on the viaduct to the south-west, so the kit's numbers run the other way from the real ones, as its note says.
- The bays' platform canopies run on beyond the train shed's glazed ends on columns (shed-1, throat-1).
- 13/14 is open at its ends, with brick parapets, and its canopy covers only the middle (platform-14-1, platform-13-14-west-1).
- The shed's side wall is buff brick with red-brick bands and round-arched windows (shed-2, shed-3).
