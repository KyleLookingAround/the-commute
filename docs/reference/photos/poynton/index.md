# Poynton

A listed 1887 LNWR station on the electrified Stockport–Macclesfield line, in a cutting below Chester Road (A5149). The red-brick booking office with stone dressings and tall chimneys stands at road level by the car park; covered stairs lead down to two platforms with canopies, kept with flowers like a heritage line; a road bridge crosses at the north end.

## What the photos settle

- The booking office is a separate listed building at road level by the car park, above the platforms.
- Both platforms have short canopied buildings near the stairs, at the road-bridge end.
- There is a station car park off the approach from Chester Road.
