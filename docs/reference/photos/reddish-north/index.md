# Reddish North

Two side platforms on the Hope Valley line through Reddish, Manchester to Marple and Sheffield. Platform 1 has the brick station building with a valanced canopy on iron columns; a lattice footbridge spans both lines at one end, with an overbridge beyond it.

## What the photos settle

- The building stands on platform 1 only; platform 2 has a shelter, no building.
- There is a small car park on the forecourt at the end of the building, off Station Road.
- The way across is a lattice footbridge with steps, off the platform ends' side rather than mid-platform; no lifts are visible.
- The canopy runs along the building's platform front only, a little past either end of it.

## Still unknown

- Which compass end the footbridge stands at: the captions don't say, and the photos show only that it is past the end of the building.
- Whether there is step-free access to platform 2.
- An aerial view: none freely licensed found.
