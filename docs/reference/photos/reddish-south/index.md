# Reddish South

On the Stockport–Denton–Guide Bridge line, long served by one 'parliamentary' train a week. One platform remains in use beside a single line, reached by steel stairs from the road bridge (Broadstone Road) at its north end; a second, long-abandoned platform lies across the trackbed. Friends of Reddish South have added murals and planting.

## What the photos settle

- One platform and one track in use; the other platform is abandoned, with its track gone.
- Access is only by stairs from the road bridge: no ramp or lift.
- No building and no shelter of note; no car park.
