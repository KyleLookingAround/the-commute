# Romiley

The junction station where the line from Reddish (via Bredbury) meets the line from Hyde (via Woodley), then runs on to Marple. Two long platforms with a crossover between them; a tall gabled brick building with a canopy on platform 1, a smaller brick building on platform 2, a footbridge, and the disused signal box at the Manchester end.

## What the photos settle

- The main building is a two-storey gabled brick building on platform 1, with a canopy on iron columns along its platform front.
- Platform 2 has its own smaller brick building with a canopy.
- There is a crossover between the two lines within the platforms, and Romiley Junction lies just beyond the signal box at the Manchester end.
- Street access is from Stockport Road (B6104), including a ramp off platform 1; the line crosses the road on a lattice-girder bridge west of the station.
