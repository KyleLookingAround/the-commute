# Rose Hill

Rose Hill Marple: the end of the line from Manchester via Hyde and Romiley. A single track with one platform ends at buffer stops; the old line on to Macclesfield is now the Middlewood Way. A small brick building with a canopy stands on the platform, a car park lies outside, and a road bridge crosses at the Manchester end.

## Round the station

The station is at the end of Railway Road off the A626 Stockport Road (Marple Road), which crosses the line on bridge 48; the Railway pub is close by and buses stop on Stockport Road.

## What the photos settle

- One platform, one track, buffers at the south end: a terminus, not a through station.
- There is a car park beside the platform, reached from Marple Road.
- The building is a small single-storey brick block with a short canopy, not a full-length canopy.
