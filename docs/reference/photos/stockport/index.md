# Stockport

The big through station south of the viaduct: island 3/4 and island 1/2, the platform 0 bay, the carriage sidings to the west and the subway under them all. The main entrance, a glass drum, faces Stockport Exchange and Grand Central on the east; a smaller entrance on the west leads into the subway, and an upper entrance leads straight on to platform 0. The town centre, the Interchange and the viaduct have their own folders under [../areas/](../areas/).

## What the photos settle

- The main entrance is the glass drum under a thin flat overhanging roof slab (entrance-east-1), as the kit's `drum` part draws it.
- Platform 4's canopy is navy with white bands on slim columns (platform-4-canopy-1).
- Platform 4 is on the west side, by the carriage sidings (platform-4-sidings-1), as the kit has it.
- The subway is the way across (subway-1); the cycle stands are by platform 0's entrance and between platforms 1 and 2 (geograph 8280256 and 8280254, not kept).
- The east side of the station is now lawns and offices (area-east-1, area-exchange-1).

## Still unknown

- Which side Station Road is on: the kit puts it on the east, but geograph 8413983's caption puts the Station Road car park on the west side of the station, and the bus stop on Station Road (area-bus-stop-1) is beside a high brick wall like the west side's. Worth checking against a map, or the view from above ([`stockport.jpg`](../../aerial/stockport.jpg)), before the kit is next touched.
- Where the taxi rank is now: geograph 5134243 (2016) says taxis no longer line up at the east entrance.
- The multi-storey car park: no free photo of it.
