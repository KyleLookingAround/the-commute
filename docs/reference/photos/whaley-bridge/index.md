# Whaley Bridge

Two side platforms on the Buxton line above the town, with stone station buildings of 1857 on both, a green lattice footbridge, and the way in through a gabled stone entrance from the street. The line crosses Reservoir Road on a bridge just beyond. Not in the game yet.
