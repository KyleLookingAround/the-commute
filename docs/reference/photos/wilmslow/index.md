# Wilmslow

A major station on the West Coast Main Line where the Styal line rejoins the Crewe line: electrified, with a long low station building and forecourt on the town side, long canopied platforms, and a glazed-roof building on the island. The line crosses the road south of the station on a girder bridge.

## What the photos settle

- The main building is long and single storey, set back behind a forecourt with a car park and a bus stop.
- Platforms have long canopies on iron columns.
