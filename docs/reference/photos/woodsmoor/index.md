# Woodsmoor

Two short side platforms on the Hazel Grove line in a wooded cutting, opened in 1990, with Moorland Road's humpback bridge at the north end where passengers come and go. The Woodsmoor Lane level crossing is just south. The newest free photos are from 2016. Positive v is east, as in the kit.

## What the photos settle

- No lifts, no footbridge: passengers come down from Moorland Road by ramps and steps (ramp-1), as the kit draws them.
- One Northern-blue shelter a platform, one short apex-roofed (platform-buxton-1) and one longer flat-roofed (platform-across-1); no canopy.
- Tall pale lamp columns and dark timber fences at the platforms' backs (platform-north-1, platform-south-1).
- The level crossing on Woodsmoor Lane just south of the station (area-woodsmoor-lane-1 and -2): not in the game yet.
