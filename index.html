Passengers arrive by the real demand curve, wait on your platforms and pay a fare when they board. Full platforms and long waits lose them. Buy the next station up the line to lift the fog: it starts as a halt, one platform for trains towards Manchester, and grows as you build it a way across, a second platform and a booking office. Progress saves on this device and catches up while you're away.
