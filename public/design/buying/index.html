Buying, rethought

Everything you can buy is a place on the map that starts small and grows in stages. Whatever touches what you own stands in the fog with its price. One card serves every place, and Strain shows where the network hurts. Static mockups, phones first: the map is drawn from the game’s own network data, and the prices are today’s or worked out from the model (drafts are marked). Nothing here is the game. The write-up: docs/design/buying.md. Back to the game.

For the owner Open questions

Each with the option the build takes if there’s no answer.

  1. Where the trains you buy live until the fleet step. On the train service’s card (tap a train): Extra unit, Longer trains and, until the line between stations, Faster timetable. The spare unit goes to Stockport’s carriage sidings, a depot. “Trains an hour” is what a player feels, and a bus route has the same shape.
  2. Which locked stations stand in the fog. Every station, as today; the ones you can buy now get a bright price and a meter, the rest a dim price shown only near the camera. Every other kind of place shows only once offered.
  3. Prices. Fixed per place in the data, today’s kept. New places drafted by rings out from Stockport, then set by hand. Prices that rise with everything owned would make Heaton Chapel dearer because you bought a bus stop.
  4. Strain: always on, or on demand. A map view beside Line, Mins and Light, off at the start and remembered. The map’s own life always shows pressure; Strain shows it all at once.
  5. Sections: bought, or come with the stations. Come with the stations at both ends; then they grow (line speed, resignalling, wires). Buying them apart doubles every step up the line and slows the opening.
  6. Contract renewal’s home. Tap the cash pill for the Network card: totals, reputation and Renew. The line panel goes.

The model

  • Places: stations, sections of line, junctions, routes (train services, bus routes, tram lines), stops (bus stops, bus stations, tram stops) and depots. Each starts small (a halt, a pole, a bus every 15 minutes) and grows in stages.
  • Touching: the next station each way on every line, a section and the stations at its ends, a stop and the station within a walk, a route and its stops. Offered once it touches what you own; real needs stay (Davenport waits on Stockport’s Platforms 0, 1 and 2).
  • Comes with: a section with the stations at both its ends, a junction with its sections, a train service with its stations, the sidings with Stockport. Never bought, only grown.
  • Today’s upgrades: all kept as stages but two. The kiosk folds into the booking office; the bus link becomes a bus stop on the map. The line upgrades go to the stopper’s card, the spare unit to the sidings.
  • The bot: saves for the offered station that brings most homes and draw per pound, which is today’s order exactly, so pacing holds.

Strain: the problems layer on

Soft warm glows, amber to coral, where it hurts: Stockport’s platform 142 of 150, people walking home from Levenshulme and Heaton Chapel (the dotted trails), and the stretch from Stockport to Heaton Chapel tinted where trains are leaving 4 min late. One card for every place

The same card for any kind of place: its name and kind, four figures for now, what’s wrong now (if anything), the stages it offers grouped by ladder (price, what it saves, what it opens next, Buy or a meter), Built, and Next to: the places that touch it, owned or offered, a tap away. The place is outlined on the map in the preview’s cyan while its card is open.

Finding places: the strip gives way

The strip of station chips becomes the Places row in the peek (above): a chip for each place owned or offered, in network order, with a kind filter once there’s a second mode. Raised to full it lists them by kind. Next to on every card walks the network across modes, and edge markers point to up to three offered places off the open map. The Station | Line switch goes: there’s one card. Tapping the cash pill opens the Network card (totals, reputation and Renew the contract).

Today, for comparison

The game as it is (npm run shots, 09:00 on day 1): the strip of station chips, one station panel and one line panel. Stockport’s halt card lists eight stages; in the new model it lists four, the bus link has moved onto the map as Stockport Interchange, and the kiosk is part of the booking hall.

Mockups are static HTML and CSS at each device’s real size, scaled to fit, so a 44 px button here is a 44 px button there. The map is the real line and roads from the game’s network data, drawn flat; fogged places, glows and the 192 are drawn where the model puts them. Figures in cards are worked out from today’s game where it has them (prices, homes, pools) and marked as drafts where the step that builds them hasn’t landed.
