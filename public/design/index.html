Recommended A · Platform sheet

One sheet that rises over the map from the bottom on phones and docks to the side on wider screens. Build this one.

  • The map stays the star. At rest the sheet is a 142 px peek, so a phone shows about four fifths map. Picking a card sinks the sheet back to that peek so its ghost is in plain view: today it sits off screen below the panel.
  • One thumb does everything. Station, cards, Buy and speed all sit in the bottom half. Only settings and cash sit up top, and settings is rare.
  • One component, every size. The same sheet is a bottom sheet in portrait and a side panel in landscape, on tablets and desktop, so stage 2 builds one thing and checks it at five sizes.
  • Leaves the map's gestures alone. Only the sheet's handle and header drag. Everything else on screen belongs to the map, as moving around the map needs.
  • Cheapest of the three. It reuses today's cards, strip and settings: medium, about $30 to build.
